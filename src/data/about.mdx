I am an MSc student in Aerospace Engineering at **Beijing Institute of Technology**, studying on a
Chinese Government Scholarship. My research is in **electric propulsion**, and in particular the
plasma sources and diagnostics behind it. I am currently designing and simulating an
[ExB probe](/research/#exb-probe) that separates the ion charge states in a thruster plume by velocity.

Before BIT, I completed a BSc in Mechanical Engineering at North China Electric Power University
(top 10 % of the class every year). For my thesis I designed, built and instrumented a
[50 A LaB₆ hollow cathode](/research/#hollow-cathode) and measured how its temperature and
discharge change with current and xenon flow.

I like work that joins hardware and modelling: designing parts in CAD, simulating fields and
particle trajectories, fabricating, and then checking the model against the experiment.

**Research interests**

- Electric propulsion and hollow cathodes
- Plasma diagnostics and ion-beam analysis
- Aircraft design and optimisation
- Structural mechanics and finite element analysis
